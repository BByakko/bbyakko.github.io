Hello! Thank you for reading my interactive fiction story! It is very much a work-in-progress right now, but I appreciate any feedback you have for me.

In [b]Rook[/b], you are a knight's squire of low-birth but well-cared for within the knight's household. Your contented life is shattered however, by the last words of the king on his death-bed. With his dying breath, he acknowledges you as his bastard son, placing your life in danger and turmoil from that moment on.

Choose whether to prove your claim, putting you on a collision course with the Crown Prince; or simply try to survive each day, while unraveling the layers of conspiracy surrounding the throne.

In this prologue, you will get a basic introduction to the setting, several characters, as well as the stats being used. Your decisions help to shape your character's traits or personality before the main chapter begins.
